        How to play

        Ask Away

        A party game of questions that never runs out. Pick a mode, and everyone answers on their own phone at the same time. Then the card flips and you see how everyone answered.

        • Party Mix: a bit of everything below, shuffled.
        • Trivia: pick a topic. A right answer scores 100, plus up to 50 more for answering fast.
        • Would You Rather and Hot Takes: pick a side. Siding with the most people scores 100 (a tie scores nothing).
        • Never Have I Ever: everyone starts with 5 fingers. Say “I have” and one goes down. Most fingers left wins. Be honest!
        • Most Likely To: vote for whoever fits best. Voting with the crowd scores 100, and the winner gets the title.
        • Couples: this-or-that and who’s-more-likely (score when you two agree), guess your partner’s answer, and questions to talk over.
        • Truth or Dare: each turn one player picks Truth or Dare, does it out loud (or on your call), then taps Done for 100 points, or Pass.
        • Spicy adds flirty questions for grown-ups. They’re cheeky, never explicit.
        • Questions are built fresh from thousands of facts and prompts, and your phone remembers what you’ve seen, so you won’t get repeats for a long time. Some trivia can come from the free Open Trivia Database (shared under CC BY-SA 4.0).
        • After a reveal, the next card comes on its own, or as soon as everyone taps Next.
